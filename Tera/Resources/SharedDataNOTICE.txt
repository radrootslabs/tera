as its country-relationship source. No country relationships are included here.

snapshot published 2026-09-17. The XML and ISO standard text are not distributed.
No cash/display rounding, exchange rate, payment rule or currency conversion
is implied. The registry is fixed; replacement requires explicit versioned
contract and provenance review. Derived artifacts must include this material
and its license/notice before adopting it.
